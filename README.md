# Wildlife Mod 🦌🐒🦉

A vanilla-friendly Minecraft mod for **Java Edition 26.1.1** that adds realistic wildlife mobs across all biomes.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft**: Java Edition 26.1.1
- **Forge**: 63.0.1 or higher
- **Java**: 25 or higher

## Animals (12 Species!)

### 🌲 Forest & Plains
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🦌 Deer** | Shy, flees from players, males have antlers, grazes | Venison, Deer Hide |
| **🐗 Boar** | Aggressive when attacked, charges | Boar Meat, Leather |

### 🌴 Jungle
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🐒 Monkey** | Climbs trees, sits when idle, 3 color variants | Monkey Fur, Cocoa Beans |
| **🦛 Tapir** | Swims, wallows in water, peaceful | Leather, Meat |
| **🦜 Toucan** | Flies between trees, perches on branches | Toucan Feather |

### 🏜️ Desert & Savanna
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🦊 Meerkat** | Stands upright to watch for danger, digs | Feather |
| **🦃 Ostrich** | Fast runner, head-bobbing animation | Ostrich Meat, Feathers |

### 🌊 Water & Wetlands
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🦦 Otter** | Swims gracefully, floats on back, playful | Otter Fur, Fish |
| **🦫 Beaver** | Builds dams, holds wood, swims | Beaver Fur, Wood |
| **🦆 Duck** | Swims, floats, quacks | Duck Meat, Feathers |

### 🏔️ Mountains & Taiga
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🐿️ Marmot** | Stands upright, hibernates in winter | Fur |

### 🌙 Night
| Animal | Behavior | Drops |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **🦉 Owl** | Flies silently, perches, hunts at night | Owl Feather |

## Features

- **Realistic AI**: Each animal has unique behaviors
- **Biome-specific spawning**: Animals spawn in appropriate biomes
- **Breeding**: All animals can be bred with appropriate foods
- **Custom models & animations**: Smooth, vanilla-style animations
- **Items**: Meat, furs, feathers, and spawn eggs

## Breeding Guide

| Animal | Food |
|--------|------|
| Deer | Wheat, Apple |
| Boar | Carrot, Potato |
| Monkey | Apple, Sweet Berries, Cocoa Beans |
| Otter | Cod, Salmon |
| Owl | Rabbit, Chicken |
| Beaver | Oak Log, Birch Log |
| Meerkat | Spider Eye, Carrot |
| Duck | Wheat Seeds, Melon Seeds |
| Toucan | Sweet Berries, Melon |
| Ostrich | Wheat Seeds, Melon Seeds |
| Tapir | Apple, Carrot |
| Marmot | Carrot, Potato |

## Building

```bash
./gradlew build
```

## Installation

1. Install Forge 63.0.1+ for Minecraft 26.1.1
2. Place the mod JAR in your `mods` folder
3. Launch the game!
